Quick Start Guide

Add a Patient

Start by adding your first patient to the system.

  • Click "Patients" in the sidebar
  • Click "Add Patient"
  • Fill in patient details

Schedule Appointment

Book appointments for your patients.

  • Click "Appointments" in the sidebar
  • Click "Add Appointment"
  • Select patient, dentist, and date

Generate Invoice

Create bills for completed appointments.

  • Go to "Billing" in the sidebar
  • View appointment details
  • Click "Generate Bill"

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new patient?

Navigate to the Patients page from the sidebar, click the "Add Patient" button, and fill in the required information including name, contact details, and medical history.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Go to the Appointments page, click "Add Appointment", select a patient, dentist, treatment type, and choose a date and time that works for everyone.

How do I generate a bill?

After an appointment is completed, go to the Billing section, find the appointment, and click "Generate Bill". The system will calculate the total based on treatment and consultation fees.

How do I manage staff members?

Only administrators can manage staff. Go to the Staff page to add, edit, or deactivate staff members.

How do I view reports?

Navigate to the Reports page to view daily, weekly, and monthly reports on patients, appointments, and revenue.
